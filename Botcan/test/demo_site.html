What the Ström is made of

Fourteen years of tooling, one wind tunnel, and a refusal to carry anything that does not earn its mass.

  • Dry-sumped V12 — 9,000 rpm, individually finished
  • T700 carbon tub — cured in a single 14-hour cycle
  • Active aero — 1,100 kg of downforce, no wing delete
  • Weight above the axles — 53%, within a millimetre of the brief
  • Hand-built — 412 hours per chassis

Feb 2026

The fourteen-hour cure, and why we stopped trying to shorten it

A single T700 tub used to take three days and a nervous production team. The current process takes fourteen hours and produces a stiffer, lighter part, and we only worked that out by accident.

Nov 2025

1,100 kg of downforce, and the argument about the wing

Our aero team wanted the rear wing deleted. Our customers did not. A compromise was found that adds 41 kg and, in our judgement, keeps the car the faster of the two.
